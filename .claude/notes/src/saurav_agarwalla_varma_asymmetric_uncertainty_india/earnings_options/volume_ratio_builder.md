# volume_ratio_builder.py

- Options volume is notional rupees ((strike + premium) x quantity), which the bhavcopy reports directly. On 14 July 2017 this gave an O/S of 1.2 and an O/F of 0.44, on the paper's scale. P/C is in contracts and, as a check, in notional value.
- The paper's Table 3 has a P/C mean of 0.124, but its Figure 5 has levels of 0.44 to 0.56. Contracts give 0.44 to 0.54 on average in 2015-2019, which matches the figure, so contracts are the main P/C.
